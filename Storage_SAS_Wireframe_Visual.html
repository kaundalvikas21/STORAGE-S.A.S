Silo map

Read this first

Three commercial axes run at once inside the storage silo — segment, size and location — because those are the three ways a customer arrives at the problem. Mudanzas sits alongside as a supporting silo that feeds traffic inward.

Why three axes and not one

Someone who has just sold a flat searches by size. Someone running an online shop searches by segment. Someone who lives in Usaquén searches by location. A single flat services page serves one of those three and loses the other two. Each axis is a separate entry point into the same commercial offer, and the internal linking pulls all of them toward the same quote form.

This is the single biggest fix on the site. The current hero, the category blocks, the sede banner and the bodega cards are all PNG images. Search engines index text, not pixels — so the highest-value page on the domain has almost nothing readable on it. Everything in this hero must be live HTML.

2

Three doors, not a services list. Each card is the entry to one silo. Bodegaje is visually dominant because it is the strategic priority.

6

10

Careful — protect what already works

The homepage currently ranks at position 9 for “bodegaje bogotá” as well. Once the dedicated pillar at /bodegaje-bogota/ is live, two pages on the same domain will be competing for that term.

Resolution: retarget the homepage title toward brand and “storage”, let the pillar own “bodegaje”, and watch both positions for sixty days after launch. If the pillar has not overtaken the homepage in that window, revisit rather than adding more pages on top.

The three axes appear as three separate choosers. This is the whole architecture expressed on one page. Every card is an internal link downward into the silo — which is how a pillar passes authority to its children.

5

Written as capability, never as warning. The discovery workbook explicitly asks to avoid messaging that creates a sense of insecurity. Describe what the security does, not what could happen without it.

7

Price ranges belong on the page. All four People Also Ask questions on the “bodegaje bogotá” results page are price questions. The brand rule is not to compete on price — but refusing to discuss price at all sends the visitor back to Google to find someone who will.

9

Two CTAs, different temperatures. “Cotizar” for the ready buyer, “Calcular espacio” for the visitor who does not yet know what they need. Most first-time storage customers are the second kind.

The mudanzas pillar — one deliberate exception to the linking rules

The same template serves /mudanzas-bogota/, with one extra block between steps 6 and 7: a bridge that reads roughly “¿Tu mudanza necesita almacenamiento temporal?” and links into the bodegaje silo.

That link runs one direction only. Mudanzas into bodegaje, never back. People who are moving are the highest-intent storage audience there is, and mudanzas terms carry roughly four times the search volume of storage terms in this market — so the moving silo becomes an acquisition channel for the business the client actually wants to grow.

Where the real opportunity sits

On the “bodegaje bogotá” results page, positions one, two and three are all local pack listings — and none of them belong to the big three competitors. They are small operators with 9, 2 and 18 Google reviews respectively.

The top of the most commercially relevant results page in this market is currently held by businesses with almost no review volume. Storage S.A.S has seven physical sites and no claimed profiles. Claiming them, populating them properly and building a steady review flow is the cheapest available route to that position — and it does not depend on the website at all.

5–6

Why this matters more than anything else here. Today every call to action on both domains is a raw WhatsApp deep link. That has three consequences, and the discovery workbook names all three as problems in its own words.

i

Attribution dies at the click. The client's stated most important measurement goal is knowing which campaign, ad and keyword produced each customer. A raw WhatsApp link destroys that information at the exact moment it is created.

ii

No qualification. The workbook reports that most prospects are lost “en conversaciones” — precisely the stage a raw WhatsApp lead lands in, with an advisor who knows nothing about them yet.

iii

No speed advantage. Response times are reported as sometimes exceeding twenty minutes. Four fields arriving with the lead let an advisor open with an answer instead of a question.

4

The WhatsApp experience is kept, not removed. This market expects WhatsApp and taking it away would cost conversions. The form sits in front of it, not instead of it — the visitor still ends up in WhatsApp, and the business gains attribution and context on the way.

R

Linking rules

Non-negotiable
#RuleWhy
1Vertical linking onlyA pillar links down to its children. Children link back up to the pillar and sideways only within the same silo. Cross-silo connections happen through the header and footer, not through body copy. This is what makes a silo a silo rather than a sitemap.
2One bridge, one directionMudanzas pages link into bodegaje through a single block. Never the reverse. People who are moving are the highest-intent storage audience there is — and mudanzas terms carry roughly four times the search volume of storage terms in this market.
3Sede pages carry proofReal address, embedded map, real photographs, opening hours, available sizes, LocalBusiness structured data. A page that cannot carry all of those should not be built.
4No speculative zone pagesBuild a location page only where a physical site serves that area with genuinely distinct content. Mass-produced near-identical location pages are what Google's spam policies describe as doorway pages.
5Calle 197 gets structural priorityHeader nav entry, homepage module, featured slot on the sedes hub, first position in every sede list. It is the newest site, has the most capacity and the lowest occupancy. Structural priority costs nothing and works every day.
6Every page carries real HTML textImages may illustrate. They may not carry the message. This is the single biggest fix on the current site and the reason a five-page domain ranking at position 3 for “storage” is still invisible for almost everything else.
7Every CTA goes through the quote formNot to a raw WhatsApp link. Attribution, qualification and advisor context all depend on this one rule holding site-wide.
One honest caveat before this goes to build

Nothing in this document is a ranking guarantee. Search results depend on competitor behaviour, Google algorithm changes and factors nobody controls. Search volume figures are third-party estimates that move month to month.

What this structure does is make the site eligible to compete for terms it currently cannot reach at all — because no page exists to serve them. A domain with five pages can rank for five pages' worth of intent. That is the constraint being removed here.
